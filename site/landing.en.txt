p_zero = Three deals, and nobody needed money to start. The bottom row sums to zero, and always will: a unit is created by the buyer when buying and disappears when that member sells. There is no issuer, no interest, nothing to mine and nothing to invest in.
h_diff = What makes this one different
c1_h = Your keys, on your devices
c1_p = Every payment is signed by a key made on your phone or computer that never leaves it. Nobody, the operator included, can spend for you.
c2_h = No administrator of the whole
c2_p = Each community runs its own node with its own rules and governs itself. Communities connect by trust lines they choose.
c3_h = You check the books
c3_p = The wallet verifies every record that concerns you and can prove it if a node ever lies.
c4_h = Beside money
c4_p = Prices stay in the national currency. A deal can be part units, part money. Taxes are paid in money, as for any sale.
h_stands = Where it stands
note = It is not a cryptocurrency, not network marketing and not a way around taxes. It proves who promised what; it cannot make anyone keep a promise. That rests on people knowing each other, on vouching, and on limits that grow with a record of keeping one's word.

p_stands = The ledger rules, signed account chains, a community's node, a public hub where anyone creates a community, trust lines by which communities trade with each other, and this wallet run and are tested. Family and company accounts and payments through a third community are designed and not yet built. No real community uses it yet, and it has not been reviewed by a lawyer or a tax adviser. The <a href="https://github.com/okay-effects/okay-mutual#what-runs-today-and-what-is-designed">README</a> separates what runs from what is designed.
h_node = Start your own community
p_node = Create one in the wallet in a minute: no server, no terminal. Your device's key makes you its founder; you admit members and set their limits. Or run the whole thing yourself, on Cloudflare or on your own machine: the code is open (Apache-2.0). See the <a href="https://github.com/okay-effects/okay-mutual/blob/main/docs/run-a-node.md">guide</a>. A demo community, Demo Town, is open to try.
