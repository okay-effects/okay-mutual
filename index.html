Trade when money is short but work, goods and needs are not.

Okay Mutual is an open network of mutual credit. People and businesses sell to and buy from each other for units they issue to one another. It runs beside ordinary money and replaces nothing.

Open your account Read the idea

Keeping count is all it takes

A tailor has free hours, a baker has bread, a programmer would build the tailor a booking page. Each waits for money to arrive first. Give each an account that starts at zero, and let them trade:

What happensBakerTailorProgrammer
the tailor alters the baker's jacket for 80−80+800
the programmer builds the tailor's page for 200−80−120+200
the baker supplies the programmer's office for 150+70−120+50

Three deals, and nobody needed money to start. The bottom row sums to zero, and always will: a unit is created by the buyer when buying and disappears when that member sells. There is no issuer, no interest, nothing to mine and nothing to invest in.

What makes this one different

Your keys, on your devices

Every payment is signed by a key made on your phone or computer that never leaves it. Nobody, the operator included, can spend for you.

No administrator of the whole

Each community runs its own node with its own rules and governs itself. Communities connect by trust lines they choose.

You check the books

The app on your device verifies every record that concerns you and can prove it if a node ever lies.

Beside money

Prices stay in the national currency. A deal can be part units, part money. Taxes are paid in money, as for any sale.

Where it stands

The ledger rules, signed account chains, a community's node, a public hub where anyone creates a community, trust lines by which communities trade with each other, and the app run and are tested. Family and company accounts and payments through a third community are designed and not yet built. No real community uses it yet, and it has not been reviewed by a lawyer or a tax adviser. The README separates what runs from what is designed.

It is not a cryptocurrency, not network marketing and not a way around taxes. It proves who promised what; it cannot make anyone keep a promise. That rests on people knowing each other, on vouching, and on limits that grow with a record of keeping one's word.

Start your own community

Create one in the app in a minute: no server, no terminal. Your device's key makes you its founder; you admit members and set their limits. Or run the whole thing yourself, on Cloudflare or on your own machine: the code is open (Apache-2.0). See the guide. A demo community, Demo Town, is open to try.
